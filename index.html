About the Project

SMART AGRICULTURE

Smart Irrigation Control Center is an IoT-based irrigation system designed to monitor soil and environmental conditions and intelligently control a water pump. An ESP8266 reads the soil-moisture sensor and DHT11 temperature/humidity sensor, while the local relay, LEDs and LCD provide immediate physical feedback. Blynk Cloud connects the controller to this professional dashboard for remote monitoring and control. In AUTO mode, irrigation is controlled from the configured soil thresholds; in MANUAL mode, an authorized operator can control the pump from the dashboard.
